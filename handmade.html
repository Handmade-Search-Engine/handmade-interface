The Search Engine

This is a search engine made to find humans rather than information. Search about your interests and find people who share them.

We need the help of developers, artists, writers, and everyone who wants a more human internet.

You can contribute to the project on GitHub.

You can find more details on how you can help in the contribution guidelines.
